  title: "Numerical Solution of Diffusion Equations with Material Interfaces: Application to Tissue Engineering Problems"
  title_original: "Risoluzione Numerica di Equazioni di Diffusione in Presenza di Interfacce Materiali: Applicazione a Problemi di Ingegneria dei Tessuti"
  summary: >-
    Starting from a problem proposed by my thesis advisor, I modeled oxygen
    diffusion and consumption by chondrocytes, the cells that produce
    cartilage, in an in-vitro culture. The 1D model couples two domains, the
    nutrient medium and the cell biomass, through a biological interface. I
    discretized it with finite differences on a uniform grid, handling an
    interface that need not fall on a grid node, and implemented the solver in
    MATLAB with convergence and error analyses. I then extended it to a
    time-dependent problem based on real laboratory data.
