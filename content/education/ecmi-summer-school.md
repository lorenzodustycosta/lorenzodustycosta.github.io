project:
  title: "Heat in a Battery: Model of a Cylindrical Lithium-Ion Battery"
  summary: >-
    Overheating from short circuits or overcharging is the main cause of
    lithium-ion battery fires. In a team of six international students, I
    contributed to every phase of the project as we modeled heat generation and
    diffusion in a cylindrical cell, treating the battery as a power source
    with internal resistance (Joule heating). The model solves the heat
    equation in cylindrical coordinates with radial symmetry, accounting for
    the different thermal properties of each layer (electrodes, separators,
    copper and aluminium collectors, external can). We discretized it with the
    Crank–Nicolson scheme, which reduces each time step to a linear system.
skills: [mathematical-modeling, pde, finite-differences, crank-nicolson, teamwork]
