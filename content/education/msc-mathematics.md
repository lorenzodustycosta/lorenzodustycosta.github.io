  title_original: "Analisi Teorica e Aspetti Computazionali per Problemi di Piastre con Applicazioni all’Ingegneria Sismica"
  summary: >-
    I studied the Reissner–Mindlin plate model applied to earthquake
    engineering, using linked interpolation finite elements to overcome shear
    locking in thin plates. I analyzed the convergence proof for a triangular
    element under suitable assumptions, and also examined a rectangular one.
    During a six-month internship at EUCENTRE (European Centre for Training and
    Research in Earthquake Engineering), I optimized their Fortran FEM code,
    which was too slow or unusable on fine meshes. Using band storage for the
    system matrices and a Lanczos variant for the generalized eigenvalue
    problem, I cut the run time on the largest grid the old code could handle
    (90 elements per side) from over 3.5 hours to under 3 seconds. The new
    solver handles grids of 500 elements per side in under 3 minutes and
    enables modal analysis on much finer meshes. I validated it on a
    multi-material building wall with openings under wind and seismic loads.
