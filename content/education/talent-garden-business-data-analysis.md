project:
  title: "Brand reputation model for ENI gas stations in Italy"
  summary: >-
    Group project commissioned by ENI to build a data-driven brand reputation
    model. We focused on the group's gas stations, since their data could be
    collected through the Google Maps API, which was free at the time. I led
    the technical side end to end, in Python, and the project management; my
    teammates covered the business side and the final presentation, and
    everyone took part in the manual tagging. I collected address, coordinates
    and reviews of 1,500+ stations and tagged 10,000+ reviews. I trained an SVM
    classifier to extract review topics (67% accuracy), used the Dandelion API
    for sentiment analysis, and presented the results in Tableau.
